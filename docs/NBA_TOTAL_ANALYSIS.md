# NBA full-game total analysis

The production NBA Reader screen calls an authenticated `/api/nba/analysis`
endpoint for each matched prestart full-game total. The endpoint reads its
price from the saved Reader board and its event from the validated NBA schedule;
clients cannot submit prices, scores, historical features or lineup inputs.
It verifies the quote again before returning. Expired/changed quotes, started
games, identity conflicts and locked/missing totals have no usable analysis.

`nba-total-pace-rest-v1` preserves the recent offense/defense score predictor and
its prior paired bias correction from the independent research site. It adds
the fixed 30-day weighted, ridge-20 total residual correction using prior team
pace (last five per team) and archived date-gap proxies. Historical residuals
are frozen prequentially; same-day and future outcomes do not enter a target.
Per-team scores remain separate from the corrected total.

The immutable server training artifact contains 1,315 verified score/pace rows
and all 1,243 ready residual observations, including warmup observations. It is
generated by `scripts/prepare-nba-analysis-training.mjs` from the saved research
source and records source/plan/implementation hashes. The artifact currently
covers season-ending-year 2026 regular and postseason. The model requires the
target's same season-ending year and game type, three prior games per team and
50 earlier residual observations. Season 2027 or preseason data are insufficient
until a compatible verified training artifact is supplied; old regular-season
observations are never silently substituted.

This deployment provides a forecast/reference interface, not a newly validated
betting strategy. The archive was recovered after the games; injury and pregame
lineup features remain absent. Historical distribution cash estimates are not
certified pregame EV or calibrated current win probabilities. The research
49.05% rate is not used as a game's win probability. `executable` and
`promotionEligible` remain false. Full-game spreads and first-half markets keep
their Reader display; they do not inherit this total correction. Baseball
analysis registries, ranking, hashes and betting ledgers are unchanged.

Historical research and downloads stay at the independent research site.
The production screen does not import the historical win-rate dashboard.
